## Where the item library comes from

`models/library.json` (1,461 names, 2,141 part-list designs) holds designs from two sources: converted from the Maris3D parts data
(Apache-2.0, same copyright holder), and drafted by large language models from item briefs, then validated and repaired by this
project's scripts (size, part count, colour checks). The prompts and scripts that produced them are not part of this
repository. The designs are released under Apache-2.0 together with the rest of the repository.

All product names belong to their owners. Terraville is an independent project.
